## English listing

### Short description (73/80 characters)

Daily Hindu Panchang, Muhurat, Rahu Kaal, Hora, Choghadiya and festivals.

### Full description

Plan your day with Sanatan Watch, a Hindu Panchang and Muhurat app inspired by Sanatan traditions. Check daily Panchang details, important timings, festivals and devotional reading in one place.

**Daily Panchang**
View Tithi, Vara, Nakshatra, Yoga, Karana, Paksha, sunrise, sunset, moonrise and moonset. Choose a date and city or use your location for local timings.

**Muhurat and time cycles**
Check Rahu Kaal, Yamaganda, Gulika Kaal, Abhijit Muhurat, Brahma Muhurat, Hora and day/night Choghadiya. The live Sanatan time-wheel brings current Hora and Choghadiya periods together in a clock-style view.

**Festivals and devotional practice**
Explore Hindu festivals and vrat dates, read selected stotras, and use the mantra-japa counter and devotional reminders to support your daily practice.

**Made for everyday use**
Core Panchang calculations run on your device, so the app is designed to work offline after installation. Create and share a Panchang poster, and switch between Hindi and English in the app.

Panchang and Muhurat times depend on the selected date, location and calculation conventions. Small differences may occur across regional Panchang traditions and due to local conditions.
