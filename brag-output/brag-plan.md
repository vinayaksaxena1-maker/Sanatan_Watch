# Brag Plan: Sanatan Watch (सनातन घड़ी)

## What is this app?
Sanatan Watch (सनातन घड़ी) is the world's most advanced, high-precision Vedic Timekeeper and Panchang engine, combining ancient Indian Kalachakra science with Swiss Ephemeris C-WASM calculations — 100% offline with zero latency.

## The angle
Forget generic smartwatch clocks. Every moment has a cosmic quality: Amrit, Shubh, Labh, or Rahu Kaal. Sanatan Watch visually maps the 16 Chaughadiyas, 24 Horas, 28 Anandadi Yogas, and 100+ Hindu festivals in real-time right on your screen.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Deep cosmic dark space with sacred golden particle ring expanding. A bold headline drops with impact: "WHAT IF TIME WASN'T JUST NUMBERS... BUT COSMIC ENERGY?" Cut straight into the glowing Golden & Saffron Analog Dial.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Moment 1 (3-7s): The Living Chaughadiya & Hora Clock.** The circular ring glows in real-time, instantly highlighting whether the current muhurat is Amrit (immortal/best), Shubh (auspicious), or Rahu Kaal (avoid).
- **Moment 2 (7-12s): 35-Point Vedic Panchang Engine.** Tithi, Nakshatra, Yoga, Karana, 28 Anandadi Yogas, and Ishtakala with exact ending moments down to the second.
- **Moment 3 (12-16s): 100+ Festivals & Auspicious Muhurats.** Real-time suitability scoring for Griha Pravesh, Vivah, and Vahan Kharid with 12-month Vrats & Jayantis.

## Outro / punchline (16-20s)
"Stop checking the time. Start living the Muhurat." The sacred OM and official Sanatan Watch crest settles with download buttons and the domain `sanatanwatch.in`.

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry:** App opens with glowing saffron Kalachakra dial showing live active Chaughadiya.
2. **Key Action:** Swiping into Panchang details revealing planetary degrees & 28 Anandadi Yogas.
3. **Result:** Instant green/red suitability score for major life events.
