Privacy Policy for Samay Ghadi

Effective Date: July 9, 2026

Samay Ghadi is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information.

1. 100% Offline and Local Processing

Samay Ghadi is designed as an offline-first application. All calculations, including Vedic Panchang, Muhurats, Hora, and Choghadiya cycles, are calculated directly on your device. We do not use any external APIs or cloud services for calculations.

2. Information Collection and Usage

  • Location Data (GPS/City Selection): The app requires location coordinates (latitude and longitude) to calculate precise local sunrise, sunset, moonrise, and moonset times. This coordinates processing happens entirely locally in your browser's background thread (Web Worker). We never transmit your location data to external servers or third parties.
  • Time and Date: System clock time is accessed locally to update active Muhurats and Hora rings in real time. This data is processed exclusively on-device.

3. Third-Party Access

Since the application operates entirely locally on your device without any network requests, no third parties have access to your personal details or location data.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via our support channels.
